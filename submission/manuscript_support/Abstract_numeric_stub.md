# Abstract numeric stub — not final prose

Among 504 day-1 SIC patients, delayed-entry longitudinal RNA and plasma-protein analyses were linked to 60-day mortality.
The day-5 RNA sensitivity estimand included 487 patients in positivity-supported centres; 320 had observed RNA and 53 subsequent deaths.
IPW diagnostics were classified as caution (ESS/observed=0.913; maximum weighted |SMD|=0.450), but weighted and unweighted Hallmark profiles remained highly concordant (NES Spearman=0.980; direction agreement=95.9%; significant-set Jaccard=0.88).
The frozen biological conclusion is stage-specific RNA prognostic remodelling, persistent plasma extracellular-matrix/tissue-remodelling signals and selective interferon-centred cross-compartment coupling.
