# Data and licensing boundary

The source resource is [OMIX011182](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/omix/release/OMIX011182), controlled-access human clinical and multi-omic data. Requesters must obtain their own approval and comply with applicable data-use conditions. This code archive supplies no right to redistribute participant-level inputs or derivatives.

The archive includes non-identifying aggregate results and feature/pathway-level summaries only. Existing centre displays use anonymous aggregate labels and do not include a hospital mapping. Small-group S14 descriptions are retained without claiming absolute anonymity or proving absence of selection bias. Original approval correspondence and patient-level audits are not public assets.
