## CMAISE/OMIX011182

Participant-level clinical, whole-blood RNA-seq and plasma proteomic data are
controlled-access materials distributed by the National Genomics Data Center
under accession OMIX011182. They are not included in this repository. Access
must be obtained independently from the data custodian and remains subject to
its approval, the approved research scope and all applicable terms of use.

Dataset record:

https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/omix/release/OMIX011182

The OMIX011182 record instructs users of the data to cite:

Nature Communications. 2025;16:10328.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-65271-4 (PMID: 41285725).

Any publication based on the controlled data should identify accession
`OMIX011182` and state that the source data are available through the formal
OMIX controlled-access application process.
